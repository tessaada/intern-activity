Challenge 6: Tower of Hanoi

Instructions:

In this game of Tower of Hanoi, there are 3 towers and 5 discs of different sizes. You start with all discs on Tower 1, and the goal is to move them all to Tower 3. You can only move one disc at a time, and you can't put a bigger disc on top of a smaller disc.

Create a function that takes the number of discs as an argument and returns the minimum number of steps needed to complete the game.
