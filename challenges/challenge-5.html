Challenge 5: Fraud Detection Terminal

Instructions

A banking system has flagged a number of transactions for review. Your task is to go through the dataset and identify which ones are fraudulent.

You have been provided with a dataset here: transactions.txt

Each transaction follows this format:

TransactionID, Amount, Hour, AccountAge

Fraud Risk Rules:

  • Amount > 2000 → +1 risk point
  • Amount > 5000 → +2 risk points (this overrides the normal amount rule)
  • Transaction hour between 01:00–04:00 → +1 risk point
  • Account age < 30 days → +1 risk point

A transaction is marked as FRAUD if its total risk score is 2 or higher.

Write a program to calculate how many transactions are classified as FRAUD.
